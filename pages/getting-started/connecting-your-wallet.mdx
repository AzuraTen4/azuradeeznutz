# Connecting your wallet
Before you launch the game, we recommend connecting your testnet wallet,
because the game runs on the blockchain.<br />
For example:
- Rise Wallet
- Metamask
- OKX
- Rabbywallet
- etc.

## Delegate and Top up your sessional wallet (in-game wallet)
<TipBox>
  💡 **Tip:** You need to create your [Character](https://azuradeeznutz-guide.vercel.app/getting-started/creating-your-character) first before delegating your wallet
</TipBox>

After claiming the faucet, you can delegate and top up your sessional wallet
by simply clicking the **Delegate** button and confirming the transaction
in your testnet wallet.

Once your wallet is delegated, you can top up your sessional wallet, allowing you
to play easily without needing to confirm every transaction through your
testnet wallet.
![delegate](/gif/delegate.gif)
