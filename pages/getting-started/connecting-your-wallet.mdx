# Connecting your wallet

**In case you missing the introduction game website/link: <u>https://forthekingdom.xyz</u>**

Before you launch the game, we recommend connecting your fund wallet,
because the game runs on the fastest blockchain **(RISE Chain)**.<br />

## How to Bridging L1 ETH Mainnet To L2 RISE ETH Mainnet:
1. Prepare ETH on Ethereum Mainnet (L1).
⚠️ Important: RISE’s native bridge currently ONLY supports bridging ETH from Ethereum Mainnet.
Please do NOT use Base, Arbitrum, Ronin, etc.
2. Visit the official game website: <u>https://forthekingdom.xyz</u> . After that click wallet menu on the top-right corner
3. Connect your wallet, make sure you've the good amount of ETH On Ethereum Mainnet L1
4. Click the **BRIDGE** Button under your wallet and Enter the ETH amount you want to move from Ethereum L1 🡆 RISE L2.
5. Once everything looks good, hit **CONFIRM**. Your ETH will appear on RISE in approximately ~1 minute. 
6. Try create your FTK character and Pay the creation fee to get started.
7. Delegate you're session wallet, so you can play without constantly signing transactions, which i made the details in the next section.

## Delegate and Top up your sessional wallet (in-game wallet)
<TipBox>
  You need to create your [Character](https://azuradeeznutz-guide.vercel.app/getting-started/creating-your-character) first before delegating your wallet
</TipBox>

After claiming the faucet, you can delegate and top up your sessional wallet
by simply clicking the **Delegate** button and confirming the transaction
in your testnet wallet.

Once your wallet is delegated, you can top up your sessional wallet, allowing you
to play easily without needing to confirm every transaction through your
testnet wallet.
