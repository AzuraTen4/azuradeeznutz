# Connecting your wallet
Before you launch the game, we recommend connecting your testnet wallet, because the game runs on the blockchain.<br />
For example: 
- Rise Wallet
- Metamask
- OKX
- Rabbywallet
- etc.
## Claim testnet faucet
This game currently on testnet phase, you can claim RISE ETH testnet in <br />here https://portal.risechain.com/

## Delegate and Top up your sessional wallet(in-game wallet)
After claiming the faucet, you can delegate and top up your sessional wallet by simply clicking the Delegate button and confirming the transaction in your testnet wallet. 


Once your wallet is delegated, you can top up your sessional wallet, allowing you to play easily without needing to confirm every transaction through your testnet wallet.
