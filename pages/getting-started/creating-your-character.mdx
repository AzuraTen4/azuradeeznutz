# Creating your character
In FTK, each player is given the opportunity to create a unique character. You may customize your character appearance, develop a detailed background story, and select one of the four great kingdoms as your origin.

## Selecting your kingdom

![select](/gif/select.gif)

There is 4 Unique Kingdom in this game:
- 🌳**Slyvanreach**,
Lush woodland kingdom rich in timber and fertile fields, with people living in harmony with nature and celebrating lively harvest traditions.

- ❄️**Everfrost**,
Snow-covered realm of hardy forests and quality hides, shaped by resilient craftsmen and enduring hunting culture.

- 🏜️**Sunscar**,
Sun-scorched desert kingdom abundant in stone and ore, forged by relentless heat and sustained by skilled miners and builders.

- 🔮**Misthaven**,
Mist-filled land of cliffs, caves, and rich fisheries, where resilient people thrive through stonecraft and deep connection to sea and fog.

Each Kingdom has their own Advantages and disadvantages 

## Customize your character

![create](/gif/create.gif)

Create your own character background story. Each randomly generated question you answer will determine your character starting stats.
