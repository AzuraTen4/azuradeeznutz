# Basic Gameplay
In FTK, your adventure is shaped by four main activities: exploring the map, hunting monsters, gathering resources, and engaging in PvP battles.

## Move
This game uses a tile-based system like chess, but you can move freely in any direction. Simply tap the tile you want to go to, then press the **Move Here** button. A timer ⌛ will appear when moving between tiles, which can be reduced by increasing your **🥾 Move Speed attribute**.

![move](/gif/move.gif)
<br />
## Hunt Monster
After moving to your selected tile, open the Field Map icon in the bottom tab or press F and select the monster you want to hunt. Make sure to check its stats & Loot that will drop, & Then press **Hunt**.

If the hunt is successful, you will receive loot, character EXP (for leveling up), and equipment perk EXP (based on the weapon or armor your using). You will also unlock a farming slot for gathering resources.

![hunt](/gif/hunt.gif)
<br />
## Gathering Resources
After hunting a monster, you will receive a **Farming Slot**. You can use it to gather resources like wood, fish, wheat, and more. Just open your Field Map, select the resource you want, and press the **Collect** button and Just be sure you have the correct tool equipped for gathering.

![gather](/gif/gather.gif)
<br />
## Challenge & PvP
### Challenge:
Challenged PvP (Friendly Match): Open **Challenge** Icon in the bottom tab or press P, also you can sort the level and then press **Challenge** Button to start PvP, Nothing to lose you can simple PvP here, win or lose its not matter in Challenged PvP.

![challenge](/gif/challenge.gif)
<br />
### PvP:
Normal PvP: When you on the tiles and then open Field Map, you can see the player tab, when you open it, you can see all the player including you in the tiles. Press **Attack** button to start PvP.

![pvp](/gif/pvp.gif)
<br />
<TipBox>
 - Killing other kingdom citizens with over 1,070 Fame will grant you +10 Fame. <br />
