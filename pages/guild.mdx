# Guild
Guild is an alliance of adventurers united under one banner. Together, members grow stronger, earn glory, and rise as one of the realm most powerful factions through teamwork and shared victories.<br /> <br />
![guild](/gambar/guild.png)

## Creating a Guild
To create a Guild, you must have 10,000 Gold. Forming a guild allows you to gather allies under one banner, build a strong community, and grow together in the realm. As a Guild Master, you can manage members, approving/kicking a members. <br />
![createguild](/gif/createguild.gif) <br />

## Joining a Guild
To join a guild, simply click the Join button to send a request. Your request will remain pending until it is approved by the Guild Master. <br />
![joinguild](/gif/joinguild.gif) <br />
