# Perks
✨Perks are passive progress systems that improve your performance based on the equipment or tools you use. Each time you use a specific type of equipment or tool, You gain EXP for the perk related to it.<br />
For Example:
- If you go fishing and catch some fish, your Fisher Perk will gain EXP.
- If you fight monsters using a sword, your Sword Perk will gain EXP.

## Type of Perks:
- Equipment Perks ( ⚔️Weapons and 🧥Armor )
- Tool Perks ( 🌲🪓Lumberjack, ⛏Ore miner, 🧱Stone miner, 🌾 Farmer, 🍓Berry Collector & 🎣Fisher )
 <br /> <br />
### **Equipment Perks:**
Equipment Perks are related to Weapons & Armor you use in the battle. They include:


- ⚔️ Weapon Perks: Such as Sword, Axe, Spear, Bow, Dagger, and Staff, also Shield.
- 🧥 Armor Perks: Including Headgear, Body Armor, and Footwear <br />

To level up your Equipment Perks, open the Perk Menu and press the “Training” button on the perk you want. Make sure you also equip the matching weapon or armor.
When you fight monsters, you will get EXP for the perk you are training. If the EXP bar is full, the perk will level up, and you can craft and wear higher tier equipment.
<TipBox>
You can only train one Equipment Perk at a time.
For example: If you’re training the Dagger Perk, each kill will give you Dagger EXP. When it levels up, you can craft and equip higher-tier daggers, this applies to all other weapons and armor too.
</TipBox>
 <br /> <br /> <br />
### **Tools Perks:** 
Tool Perks are related to gathering professions like fishing, mining, and farming. Each time you perform an activity using a tool like chopping wood or catching fish you gain EXP for that specific tool’s perk.

**There are currently six tool perks in FTK:**

- 🎣 Fisher ( Fishing Rod ) : <br />
Increases as you catch fish. Higher levels let you use better fishing rods and catch rarer fish.

- 🪓 Lumberjack ( Wood Axe ) :  <br />
Level up by chopping trees. Higher levels allow you to equip stronger axes and collect higher-tier wood.

- ⛏ Ore Miner ( Pickaxe ) : <br />
Gain EXP by mining ores. Higher levels unlock better pickaxes and let you gather rare ores.

- 🧱 Stone Miner ( Hammer ) : <br />
Gain EXP by mining stone. Higher levels mean more durable tools and better-quality stone.

- 🌾 Farmer ( Sickle ) : <br />
Harvest wheat to gain EXP. As you level up, you’ll use stronger tools and harvest better-quality wheat.

- 🍓 Berry Collector ( Berry Shears ) : <br />
Collect wild berries to gain EXP. Higher levels allow you to use better shears and collect rare berries.
