# Rebirth
Rebirth is a feature that lets you start over from level 99 back to level 1, but without losing your progress. You keep all your items, gear, and perks, only your level is resets. As a bonus, each time you Reborn, you gain **+20 Attribute Points that you can use to boost your Attack, Defense, or Agility.**

![99](/gambar/99.png)

It’s a replayability system that offers a fresh experience with each playthrough. Every time you Reborn, the game presents new challenges and unique ways to play, keeping things exciting no matter how many times you start over.

<TipBox>
⚠️ Each time you Reborn, the number of required Slime Boss loot increases by +1.
For example:
- Reborn 1 → Requires 1 of each Slime Boss Loot ( Total of 4 Items )
- Reborn 2 → Requires 2 of each Slime Boss Loot ( Total of 8 Items )
- Reborn 3 → Requires 3 of each Slime Boss Loot ( Total of 12 Items )
…and so on.
