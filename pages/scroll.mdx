# Scrolls
Scrolls are one-time-use consumable items. These items grant temporary stat buffs, such as increased attack, defense, or agility. Scrolls can also be used on enemies to apply debuffs or deal damage. <br /> 
![scroll](/gambar/scroll.png)<br /> <br /> 
Each scroll has its own unique type and effect
## Buff Scrolls
You can apply up to two Buff Scrolls to yourself at the same time.
![buff](/gif/buffscroll.gif)<br /> <br /> <br /> 


## Debuff/Damage Scrolls
You can apply up to two debuff scrolls to a single enemy.
Damage scrolls can target one enemy or multiple enemies, depending on the scroll. Some scrolls affect a single target, while others affect multiple targets.
![debuff](/gif/debuff.gif)<br /> <br /> <br /> 

![quick](/gambar/qab.png)
<TipBox>
You can use the Quick Access Bar to easily use scrolls by pressing the star icon on the item. You can switch to the Quick Access Bar by pressing TAB or clicking the ⇄ icon at the bottom of the screen.
