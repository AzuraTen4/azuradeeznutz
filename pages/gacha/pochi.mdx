# Pochi (Pet) It's ready.

What is Pochi?
Pochi is a cute little friend that following you around in the Outer World, Each type is giving the player different advantage, so collect them all! It's designed to helping players explore and progress throughout this adventure! <br />
![eggpochi](/gambar/pochiegg.png)

There are currently 3 types of Pochi:
### 🥚 Cotochi: 
A Pochi in a soft, fluffy, egg-like shape.
![coto](/gambar/cotochi.png)

### 📦 Pakochi: 
A Pochi shaped like a winged box. Looks like chocolate box 🍫
![pako](/gambar/pakochi.png)

### 🐉 Drakochi: 
A Pochi in the form of a small dragon.
![drako](/gambar/drakochi.png)

## How to get it?
You can get pochi by buying the egg via **Wish** menu, you can click or press w to open up **Wish/Gacha Menu**, It's cost 10 Crystal Per egg but if you buy 10 it will cost 80 Crystal.
![wishmenu](/gambar/wishmenu.png)
