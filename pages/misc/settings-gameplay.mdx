# Gameplay Settings
These are few options you can enabled/disabled in the games:
- Music🔊 <br />
You can enjoy the music in this game or you can disabled it, Up to you.

- Battle Animation🐾 <br />
You can see the good animation in this game, or just skip it if you in rush. Up to you.

- Action Animation <br />
You can see the moving/gathering animation, its up to you 

- Display Nearby Players👥 <br />
You can display nearby players, or simply don’t care about nearby players, its up to you. The dots represent how many players in that tiles.

- Kingdom Territories🌎 <br />
This options will display any kingdom territories, including yours. Its up to you to enabled/disabled it.

<TipBox>
Colored Tiles means a Kingdom has been captured that area. Each kingdom has different colors, For example: Slyvanreach ( Green ) etc.
</TipBox>
