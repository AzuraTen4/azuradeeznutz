# Gameplay Settings <br />
These are few options you can enabled/disabled in the games: <br />

- Music🔊 <br />
You can enjoy the music in this game or you can disabled it, Up to you. <br />

- Battle Animation🐾 <br />
You can see the good animation in this game, or just skip it if you in rush. Up to you. <br />

- Action Animation <br />
You can see the moving/gathering animation, its up to you <br />

![settings](/gambar/settings.png) <br /> <br /> <br />

- Display Nearby Players👥 <br />
You can display nearby players, or simply don’t care about nearby players, its up to you. The dots represent how many players in that tiles. <br />
![nearbyp](/gambar/nearbyp.png) <br />

- Kingdom Territories🌎 <br />
![optworld](/gambar/optworld.png) <br />
This options will display any kingdom territories, including yours. Its up to you to enabled/disabled it. <br />
![lantaiwarna](/gambar/lantaiwarna.png) <br />

<TipBox>
Colored Tiles means a Kingdom has been captured that area. Each kingdom has different colors, For example: Slyvanreach ( Green ) etc.
</TipBox>

- Notifications🔔 <br />
This options gives you information about your last fight like PvE, PvP & Challenge. Also you can watch the replay about the fight, so you can learn what you should do in the Next battle. <br />
![notip](/gambar/notip.png) <br />
