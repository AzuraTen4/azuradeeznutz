# Achievement
Achievement is a special in-game challenge or milestone. By completing achievements, you unlock progress and get closer to becoming a Legend!
![ach](/gambar/ach.png)

Currently There’s only 20 Achievement, 16 are the Core Achievements and 4 of them are Special Achievements.
## Core Achievements
1. Citizen <br />
You can get it by creating a character in the game and choose any one of four kingdom in the game <br />
![citizen](/gambar/citizen.png)

2. Knight Of Valor <br />
You can get it by killing any bosses in the game ( This Achievement gives you +1 Attack and +1 Defend Stats point )<br />
![kov](/gambar/kov.png)<br />

3. Forest Sentinel🟨 <br />
You can get it by Completing couple of quest in **Slyvanreach Kingdom (Liliana Quest)** This Achievement gives you +1 Agility Stats Point.<br />
![sentinel](/gambar/sentinel.png)<br />

4. Champion Of The Mist🟪 <br />
You can get it by Completing couple of quest in **Misthaven Kingdom (Maeve Quest)** This Achievement gives you +1 Defend Stats Point.<br />
![cotm](/gambar/cotm.png)<br />

5. Winter Guardian 🟦 <br />
You can get it by Completing couple of quest in **Everfrost Kingdom (Marina Quest)** This Achievement gives you +1 Agility Stats Point. <br />
![wg](/gambar/wg.png)<br />

6. Desert Moon’s Keeper🟧 <br />
You can get it by Completing couple of quest in **Suncar Kingdom (Ayesha Quest)** This Achievement gives you +1 Defend Stats Point.
![dmk](/gambar/dmk.png)<br />

7. Firedrake Slayer⚔️ 🐉 <br />
You can get it by Defeating the dragon bosses also known as The Drake Boss. This Achievement gives you +1 Attack, +1 Defend, and +1 Agility Stats Point.
![fs](/gambar/fs.png)<br />

8. Ascended Soul 🌟 <br />
You can get it by doing a Rebirth when you are at level 99. You’ll go back to level 1, and you will receive this achievement. Also this Achievement gives you +3 Attack, +3 Defend, and +3 Agility Stats points.
![as1](/gambar/as1.png)<br />

9. Scalesunder🐍 <br />
You can get it by Defeating The Serpent Bosses also known as Kalyndra the Great Serpent. This achievement gives you +1 Attack, +1 Defend and +1 Agility Stats Point.
![scalesunder](/gambar/scale1.png)<br />

10. Bloodmarked <br />
You can get it by killing 20 Enemies in PvP combat outside their safe zone and have more than 1070 Fame. This Achievement gives you +1 Attack stats point.
![pvp1](/gambar/pvp1.png)<br />

11. Renowned Duelist <br />
You can get it by killing 50 Enemies in PvP combat outside their safe zone and have more than 1070 Fame. This Achievement gives you +2 Attack stats point.
![pvp2](/gambar/pvp2)<br />

12. Warlust Ascendant <br />
You can get it by killing 100 Enemies in PvP combat outside their safe zone and have more than 1070 Fame. This Achievement gives you +3 Attack stats point.
![pvp3](/gambar/pvp3)<br />

13. The Great Reaper <br />
You can get it by killing 250 Enemies in PvP combat outside their safe zone and have more than 1070 Fame. This Achievement gives you +5 Attack stats point.
![pvp4](/gambar/pvp4.png)<br />

14. Death Incarnate <br />
You can get it by killing 500 Enemies in PvP combat outside their safe zone and have more than 1070 Fame. This Achievement gives you +10 Attack stats point.
![pvp5](/gambar/pvp5.png)<br />

15. Moonblood Silencer <br />
You can get it by Defeating The Beowulf Bosses. This achievement gives you +1 Attack, +1 Defend and +1 Agility Stats Point.
![wolf1](/gambar/wolf1.png)<br />

16. Guild Member<br />
You can get it by joining any guild in the game. This achievement gives you +1 Defend and +1 Agility Stats Point. 
![gmember](/gambar/gmember.png)<br />
