# Attributes
Learn the essential Attributes before stepping into battle.

![attributes](/gambar/attributes.png)

## 8 Types of attributes:
- ❤️**HP (Health Points)**: <br />
Shows how much health your character has before getting defeated. If you defeated you will teleport back into your Kingdom

- ⚔️**Attack**: <br />
Determines how much damage you deal to the enemy. Higher attack = harder hits.

- 🛡**Defense**: <br />
Reduces the amount of damage you receive from enemy attacks. A good defense helps you stay alive longer.

- 🪽**Agility**: <br />
Determines the attack order in battle. If your Agility is 10 points higher than your opponent, you gain an extra turn and extra critical damage based on agility diff.

- 🥾**Move Speed**: <br />
Reduces the time it takes to travel across the map.
<TipBox>
💡 **Tip:** At this moment, Move Speed can “only” be increased by using equipment with “+move speed” attributes on it.
</TipBox>

- 🔰 **Barrier**: <br />
Absorb incoming damage before HP. Fully restores at the start of each battle.

- 🌟 **Fame**: <br />
Fame measures your influence and reputation as an adventurer based on the actions you take in the world.

<TipBox>
## Fame Increased(+) by:
- Capturing a tiles gives you +10 Fame
- Do a PvP combat with enemies that over 1070 Fame ( Your Fame +10, Their Fame -20 ) <br />

## Fame Decreased(-) by:
- Do a PvP combat with your allies in Safe Zone Area, The attacker get -50 Fame.
- If you get killed by enemies and you have more than 1070 Fame, your fame get stolen by the attacker (Your fame - 20, Their Fame +10)
</TipBox>


- 🎖️ **EXP**: <br />
Represent your character’s overall growth. As you earn EXP and fill the bar, you’ll eventually level up, unlocking several benefits:
1. Your HP fully restores and slightly increases <br />
2. You gain +1 Attack Points<br />
3. You receive Attribute Points to boost Attack, Defense, or Agility
