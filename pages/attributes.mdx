# Attributes
Learn the essential Attributes before stepping into battle.

## 7 Types of attributes:
- ❤️**HP (Health Points)**: <br />
Shows how much health your character has before getting defeated. If you defeated you will teleport back into your Kingdom

- ⚔️**Attack**: <br />
Determines how much damage you deal to the enemy. Higher attack = harder hits.

- 🛡**Defense**: <br />
Reduces the amount of damage you receive from enemy attacks. A good defense helps you stay alive longer.

- 🪽**Agility**: <br />
Determines the attack order in battle. If your Agility is 10 points higher than your opponent, you gain an extra turn and extra critical damage based on agility diff.

- 🥾**Move Speed**: <br />
Reduces the time it takes to travel across the map.

-🔰**!Barrier**: <br />
Absorb incoming damage before HP. Fully restores at the start of each battle.
