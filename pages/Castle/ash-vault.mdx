# Ash Vault
Ash Vault is a place where you can store unused items and exchange them for something else, like rare resources, cards and etc.

![ashvault](/gambar/ashvault.png)

## Depositing into Ash Vault
You can deposit items into Ash Vault via the Storage Unit, then navigate to the Ash Vault menu.


![depoitem](/gif/depoitem.gif)


<TipBox>
⚠️ Once you've stored an item in Ash Vault, you can't withdraw it, you can only exchange it.
</TipBox>


## Exchanging from Ash Vault
After depositing many items into Ash Vault, it's time to exchange them for valuable items by simply going to Lucy's Shop and navigating to the Exchange menu.


![exchangecard](/gif/exchangecard.gif)
