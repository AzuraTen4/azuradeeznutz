# King👑

The King system allows one player to rule one of the four kingdoms, managing things like policies, citizen behavior, peer-to-peer marketplace taxes, alliances with other kingdoms, and more. <br /> <br />
![king](/gambar/king.png) <br /> 
Each Kingdom has their unique names:
- 🌳**Slyvanreach** = The Forest King
- 🏜️**Suncar** = [The Desert King](https://x.com/vnghuylong2)
- 🔮**Misthaven** = The Swamp King
- ❄️**Everfrost** = The Ice King

## Register as a King Candidate
To register, you must have at least 2,000 Fame. Once you meet this requirement, you can register as a king candidate in the kingdom.
<br /> 
![candidate](/gif/candidate.gif) <br /> <br />

## Election Vote 
To do a Election vote, you must have at least 1020 Fame to vote a worthy King. <br />
![vote](/gif/vote.gif) <br /> <br />
