# Fame Shop ( Archive / Unavailable )
The Fame Shop is a place where you can exchange your Fame for powerful equipment, such as swords, armor, and other gear.
The more Fame you earn, the more valuable items you can unlock in the Kingdom Armory. <br />
![fameshop](/gambar/fameshop.png)<br />

To purchase equipment, you must have enough Fame and meet the required Perk and level requirements, as each item has specific conditions that must be met before it can be exchanged.
