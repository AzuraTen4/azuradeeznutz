# Weapon Skills
✴️Skills are unique abilities that can help you in combat or even dominate your enemies. They work in both PvE (Player vs Environment) and PvP (Player vs Player). Each skill has its own special effect. ( Such as Critical, DPS, or Stun etc. )

<TipBox>
 During battle, your skills will activate from left to right.
 You can rearrange the order of your skills by moving them left or right in the skill menu. This allows you to control which skill is used first during combat.
</TipBox> 

<br /><br /><br />

## 💫Skill Points (SP) System
Each weapon skill has its own “SP Cost”.
SP stands for Skill Points. Currently, you only have 3 Skill Points available per battle. Once you run out of SP, your skill automatically will turn in to normal attack, which doens’t consume any Skill Points. <br /> <br /><br />
 

## 🔓Unlocking a new unique skills 
As you level up a weapons perk, you unlock new skills related to that weapon type. Each weapon has its own unique skill set, but the perk progression works the same way.
For example, leveling your Sword Perk will give you a new stronger sword skills, just like other weapons unlock their own as you train them.
