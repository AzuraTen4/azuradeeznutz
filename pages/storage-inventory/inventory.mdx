# Inventory
🎒The Inventory is where all your items are stored, including monster loot and resources like wood, fish, and more. Every item has its own weight, and your inventory can hold up to 200 total weight.

![inv](/gambar/inventory.png)

<TipBox>
⚠️ If your inventory goes over the weight limit(200), you won’t be able to move. A warning will appear, and you will need to either discard some items or store them in a Storage.
</TipBox>
