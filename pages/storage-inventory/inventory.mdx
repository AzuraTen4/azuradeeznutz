# Inventory
🎒 The Inventory is where all your items are stored, including monster loot and resources such as wood, fish, and more. Each item has its own weight, and your inventory can hold up to 200 total weight. You can increase this capacity by using mounts, such as horses.

![inv](/gambar/inventory.png)

<TipBox>
⚠️ If your inventory goes over the weight limit(200), you won’t be able to move. A warning will appear, and you will need to either discard some items or store them in a Storage.
</TipBox>
