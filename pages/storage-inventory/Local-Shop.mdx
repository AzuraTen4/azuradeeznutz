# Local Shop
Buy/Sell your item here, The price follows a 3:1 ratio , The price goes up depending on the item tier. For Example: purchasing a Tier 4 item costs 12 gold, while selling it only give you 4 gold.
Each item type has a 200-slot limit, so once that is full, you won’t be able to sell more of that item. To Buy/Sell you need to drag the item, into your inventory (Buy) or Lucy’s inventory (Sell)
