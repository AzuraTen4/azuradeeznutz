# Discard
The Discard feature allows you to permanently delete items from your inventory—similar to throwing them into a trash bin.
Use this when you want to clear space, remove unwanted items, or tidy up your storage.

<TipBox>
Important:
Once an item is discarded, it cannot be recovered, so make sure you really don’t need it before confirming.
</TipBox>
