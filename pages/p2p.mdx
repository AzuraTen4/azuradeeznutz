# P2P Marketplace
P2P Marketplace is a player-to-player trading system.
You can either sell your items or buy items from other players. If the shop in the castle (Lucy’s Shop) is fully stocked or doesn’t have the item that you need, P2P Market is your best option.
You can even set a higher price than Lucy’s Shop if you’re selling some times. Likewise, you can place a buy order for any unique item you’re looking for.

## P2P Rules:
P2P is located at the castle, different castle, different marketplace

### Selling Order
If you willing to sell items, simply click the item from your inventory, set your desired price, and it will appear on the P2P Market list. Also you can sell directly from buy order list if you want to.

![sellorder](/gif/sellorder.gif) <br /> <br /> <br /> 


### Buying Order
There are two ways to buy items in the P2P Market. The first is to purchase directly from an available sell order. The second is to place a buy order and wait for someone to sell the item to you.
