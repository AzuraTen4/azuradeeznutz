# 📚Quest
📚 **Quests** are special tasks given by the Kingdom that guide your adventure. By completing them, they gave you rewards with Gold & experience points (EXP) to level up your characters, and Kingdom Trust. Some quests may involve defeating powerful monsters, gathering specific resources, or exploring dangerous zones, so always be prepared!

![q1](/quest/quest1.png)

It’s recommended to begin with Daily Quests, because it’s very simple and easy to do. After that, you can proceed with your Kingdom Quests. Each kingdom has its own set of tasks, so be sure to complete the quests from the kingdom you live in first. And then you can do other Kingdom Quests, its up to you.

<TipBox>
You can easily find item you want by clicking the item name in the left tab, A Light will appear to showing where the item located.
</TipBox>
