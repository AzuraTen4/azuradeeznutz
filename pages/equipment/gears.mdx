# Gears
Gears provide additional stats and bonuses that enhance your character performance.
This category includes armor, helmets, & footwear. Each piece offers different benefits depending on your build and playstyle.

**In FTK There is 3 type of gears:**

## Leather Gears
This type of gears giving you the balance stats between Attack, Hp, Defense, and agility

## Cloth Gears
This type of gears giving you more stats on atack and agility, but lack of Hp and Defense

## Plate Gears
This type of gears giving you more stats on the Hp and Defense, but lack of attack and agility

<TipBox>
 You can combo with any type of gears <br /> 
 for example Leather Armor, Cloth Headgears, Plate Footwears
</TipBox>
