# Weapons
Weapons play a major role in shaping your combat style in FTK.
Each weapon type offers different strengths, weaknesses, and playstyles, allowing you to build a character that matches your strategy.

## Weapons Type

![weapon](/gambar/weapon.png)

There’s 7 weapons including the sub-weapon in the FTK:
- 🗡️ Sword: <br />
A balanced weapon between attack and defense, but low on agility. Most of them are One-Handed Weapons, but there’s also Two-Handed Weapons in Sword.
- 🪓 Axe: <br />
Heavy weapon with strong damage, but medium on defense and agility. Most of them are One-Handed Weapons but some are Two-Handed.
- 🔱Spear: <br />
An interesting weapon, the damage is very good, but also low on defense and agility. Most of them are One-Handed Weapons but some are also Two-Handed.
- ⚔️ Dagger: <br />
Fast And Agile Weapon, good on damage and agility but lack of defense. All of them are Two-Handed Weapons.
- 🏹 Bow: <br />
Very similar to Dagger, but has little more defense. All of them are Two-Handed Weapons.
- 🧙Staff: <br />
A unique weapon that lets you cast magic spells. It has good on damage and defense, but has little agility. All of them are One-Handed Weapons.
- 🔰Shield (Sub-weapon): <br />
Shield gives you barrier stats that can absorb some damage. The Shield is a special case, it can only be equipped if your main weapon is a one-handed type (like a sword, axe, spear and staff ). If you’re using a two-handed weapon (like a dagger or bow), you can’t equip a shield.


## Weapons Color Advantage/Disadvantage

![weaponcolor](/gambar/weaponcolor.png)

In FTK, there’s a unique feature called Color Advantage/Disadvantage, which follows a rock-paper-scissors style loop, along with a neutral type.

The colors are:

- Red ( 🗡️Sword )
- Green ( 🔱Spear )
- Blue ( 🪓Axe )
- Grey ( ⚔️Dagger, 🏹Bow & 🧙Staff )

How its works:
- If the character has the upper hand and is using a one-handed weapon, +8% bonus damage and -8% damage received. 
- If the character has the upper hand and is using a two-handed weapon, +15% bonus damage and -15% damage received.
<br /><br />
- **Red weapons** deal 8% or 15% more damage against **Green**, and **Green** deals 8% or 15% less damage back. <br />
- **Green weapons** deal 8% or 15% more damage against **Blue**, and **Blue** deals 8% or 15% less damage back. <br />
- **Blue weapons** deal 8% or 15% more damage against **Red**, and **Red** deals 8% or 15% less damage back. <br />
- **Grey** weapons are neutral, deal normal damage to any **color**, its a fair 1:1 <br />
- If both weapons are the same color, damage is also normal (1:1).
for example: Red to Red, the damage will be normal no advantage or disadvantage (1:1).
