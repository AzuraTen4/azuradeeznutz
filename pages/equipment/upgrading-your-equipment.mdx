# Equipment Upgrade⚔️
Equipment Upgrade is a feature that lets you enchant your equipment like weapons and armor. This feature gives the advantage to someone that can’t craft the next tier equipment because of their low perk level. 



## Enchanting Rank
- **The Rank of Equipment Upgrade Requirement:** <br />
+1 = You need 1 piece of the same equipment <br />
+2 = You need 1 piece of the +1 same equipment <br />
+3 = You need 1 piece of the +2 same equipment <br />

<TipBox>
For now only tier 1–6 Equipment that has +3 Rank,
Tier 7 only have +2 Rank
