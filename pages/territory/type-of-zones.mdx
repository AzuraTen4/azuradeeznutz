# Zone
In FTK, Zone Are Divided by 3 type:

## 🟩♥️Safe Zone:
Safe Zone is a very safety tiles to step on, its usually near Castles 🏰. 
If you are on this tiles:
- You will get bonus 2 Movement Speed.
- You do not lose any items or fame upon death.
- Killing an ally will reduce your fame based on kingdom policy.

![safe](/gambar/safe.png)
<br /> <br />

## 🟥⚠️Dangerous Zone:
Dangerous Zone is not safety tiles to step on, its far from the Castles🏰.
If you are on this tiles:
- PvP will affect your Fame (decrease on loss / increase on win).
- You will lose Tier 5+ farming resources upon death.

![dangerous](/gambar/dangerous.png)
<br /> <br />

## ⬛💀Death Zone:
As the name it is, Death Zone are in the middle.
If you are on this tiles:
- The same rules as the Dangerous Zone apply.
- Upon death, you will also lose your two highest-rarity pieces of equipment, the weaker equipment will be permanently destroyed, while the other will drop normally

![dz](/gambar/dz.png)
