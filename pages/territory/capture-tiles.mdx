# Capture Zone
In order to expanding your kingdom territories, you need to capture the tiles/zone. Each time you successfully capture a tiles, your kingdom will be expanded, and you will gain +10 Fame as a reward.

![areazone](/gambar/areazone.png)

## Benefits of Capturing Zones
In FTK, capturing zones gives you and other players in the same kingdom these benefits:

- Gives +2 Move Speed = Reduce time to travel between tiles
- Changes 🟥⚠️Dangerous Zone Into Safe Zone🟩♥️
- Changes ⬛💀Death Zone Into Dangerous Zone🟥⚠️

## How to Capture a Zone/Tiles?
- Bring any type Tier-1 resources **(wood, stone, fish)** & **(iron ore, wheat, berries)**
- Go to area next to your own kingdom tiles

<TipBox>
If the tiles is captured by other kingdom:
- Capturing Normal tiles you need to stand there for 2 minutes 
- Capturing Death Zone tiles you need to stand there for 5 minutes
</TipBox>

- After standing about 2/5 minutes, you can capture the tiles and you'll be rewarded by +10 Fame.
