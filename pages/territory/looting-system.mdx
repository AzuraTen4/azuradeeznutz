# Looting Mechanism
In FTK, **Dangerous Zone** all tiers 5+ Farming Resources will dropped upon death, However In **Death Zone** not only your tiers 5+ farming resources but also 2 of highest rarity equipment will be dropped upon death, the weaker equipment will be permanently destroyed, while the other will drop normally.<br /> <br /> 
![loot](/gambar/loot.png)

## How to loot
- Go to the tiles that has loot in **Loot Section** 
- Open Field Map
- Select the items you want to loot & Press Loot items
