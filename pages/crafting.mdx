# Crafting 
Crafting is a place to craft tools to farm resources or equipment to battle with PvE/PvP


You can sort the list by Type, Perk, Tier, or Slot to quickly find exactly what you need. Same as Quest, You can also press the ‘Track’ button on the right top corner to highlight the required resources, making it easier to gather what you need to craft.

![craft](/gif/crafting.gif)
<br /><br /><br /><br />

After gathering the resources, you can simply press the Craft button to create the item.
